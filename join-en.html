Eric

Eric graduated from the University of Birmingham. After returning to China, real estate became his main business.
Eric has a wealth of experience in real estate projects, including housing, shopping malls, office buildings and other types of commercial real estate, involving many areas in China, such as Xuzhou, Suzhou, Hangzhou, and Wenzhou.
In view of Eric's long-term experience in the real estate industry and the steady number of real estate projects each year, HOME GALLERY can provide bulk and multiple combinations of home customization solutions for large-scale projects.
In addition, the business partners of HOME GALLERY, in addition to traditional real estate developers and decoration companies, property agencies and designer groups have also become an important part of our business cooperation.

Company culture

Customer satisfaction is the top priority of HOME GALLERY. Using the highest level of craftsmanship to create extraordinary results for the family is the driving force of HOME GALLERY's daily work from intern to management. In addition, HOME GALLERY attaches great importance to customer participation in the service process, only to understand our working methods, understand our planning process, and witness that HOME GALLERY spends a lot of time and energy to turn customers’ rooms into unique living spaces, HOME GALLERY In order to provide the best service to customers under the transparent mechanism.
There is no doubt that high-quality, personalized service and proactive employees are inseparable. As a company founded by young returnees, HOME GALLERY adopts a flat management system. In the concept of HOME GALLERY, only when our carpenters, consultants, designers, installation team and other personnel work together, can we highlight the best concept of HOME GALLERY commercial art.
Evergreen's brand needs to have excellent quality. Our principles have not changed: the highest quality, the best service and customer satisfaction.
